Groups: your daily games, sorted

For the people you play with every day. Share one link in your WhatsApp group: players tap "I'm in" and say when they're coming, check in at the court and get fair games picked for them, score live or add the result at the end, and climb the group leaderboard. Venues and academies post daily slots by level — Beginner, Intermediate, Advanced — that players join in one tap.

Sports

Badminton, pickleball, table tennis, squash, tennis, padel, volleyball, beach volleyball, throwball, sepak takraw, football, futsal, hockey, kabaddi, basketball, snooker, pool and carrom — each with its own scoring rules, from badminton's rally scoring to pickleball side-out, tennis tie-breaks, football penalties, basketball overtime and snooker ball by ball.

How it works

  • Entries: players enter online — singles, doubles or teams — with entry fees, waitlists and partner draws.
  • Events: Men's, Women's and Mixed events, age groups and levels in one tournament.
  • Draws: round-robin groups, a league, or a seeded knockout bracket — made in one tap.
  • On the day: check-ins, an order of play across courts, and umpires scoring on any phone.
  • Live: everyone follows every point, the standings and the bracket as they happen.
