Sports

Badminton, pickleball, table tennis, squash, tennis, padel, volleyball, beach volleyball, throwball and sepak takraw — each with its own scoring rules, from badminton's rally scoring to pickleball side-out and tennis tie-breaks.

How it works

  • Entries: players enter online — singles, doubles or teams — with entry fees, waitlists and partner draws.
  • Events: Men's, Women's and Mixed events, age groups and levels in one tournament.
  • Draws: round-robin groups, a league, or a seeded knockout bracket — made in one tap.
  • On the day: check-ins, an order of play across courts, and umpires scoring on any phone.
  • Live: everyone follows every point, the standings and the bracket as they happen.
