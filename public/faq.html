Questions and answers

Everything organisers and players ask about Tonament. New here? See how it works.

Getting started

Is Tonament free?

Yes — every tournament is free for up to 16 entries, with every feature for running it. Bigger events take a one-time plan for that tournament: Club (64 entries, ₹499), Open (150, ₹999) or Championship (400, ₹1,999). See Pricing.

Which sports can I run?

Badminton, pickleball, table tennis, squash, tennis, padel, volleyball, beach volleyball, throwball, sepak takraw, football, futsal, hockey, kabaddi, basketball, snooker, pool and carrom. Cricket is next — request yours on the home page.

Do I need to install an app?

No app store needed: Tonament runs in the browser on any phone, tablet or laptop. For an app icon on your phone, tap Install the app (on the home page, or in your account menu), or add it yourself — Android Chrome: ⋮ → Install app (or Add to Home screen); iPhone Safari: Share → Add to Home Screen. It then opens full screen, like an app.

Can I try it before the day?

Yes. New tournaments are private until you publish them, so you can add a few test players, make a draw and score a match. Redo the draw any time before the first point is played.

Entries

Can I bring my Google Form?

Yes. On a tournament's Players tab, choose "Import from Google Form" and pick the CSV of its responses (in the sheet: File › Download › CSV), or paste the sheet's "Publish to web" CSV link. Each category a player ticked becomes an entry, partners are paired (or marked as looking for one), duplicates are merged, and paid entries are marked paid. You see a preview before anything is added, and players aren't messaged. You can also start a new tournament from your poster or form link: we fill in the name, dates, venue, events and fees for you to check.

Do players need an account to enter?

Yes, players sign in (or create a free account) to enter online. Their entry is linked to their account, so they can see it under "My entries" and withdraw if plans change. You can still add players yourself without accounts.

What happens when entries go over my plan's limit?

Players can still enter — their entries wait unconfirmed. Upgrade the tournament (you only pay the difference) and confirm them. You can also set a maximum number of places per event; past it, players join a waitlist.

Can players without a partner enter doubles?

Yes. They tick "I don't have a partner — find me one", and you pair them up before the draw — or choose drawn partners for the whole event, a social "draw doubles".

How do entry fees work?

Set an entry fee on any event. Players pay you directly — by UPI, with their UPI app opened with the amount filled in, or in cash — and tap “I’ve paid”. You confirm each payment with one tap on the Players tab, and their place is confirmed. Tonament never handles the money. (Tournaments organised by Tonament take fees online.)

Can players withdraw?

Players can withdraw their own entry until the draw is made, unless their payment has been confirmed or they've been paired with a partner — then the organiser handles it.

Who can see players' phone numbers and emails?

Only the tournament's organisers. Everyone else sees names and results only.

Draws and scoring

Which formats can I use?

League + knockout (round-robin groups, then the top finishers go into a knockout), a league only, or a knockout only. Each event in a tournament can use its own format and scoring. League matches can also be a fixed 2 games, where 1 game each is a draw: group tables then rank on points — win 2, draw 1, loss 0.

How are groups and seeds decided?

The order of your entry list is the seeding — move players up or down with the arrows. Groups are spread evenly so top seeds are apart, and in a knockout the top seeds get any byes.

Which scoring rules are used?

Each sport's own: badminton rally scoring to 21, pickleball side-out (or rally) scoring with the score call, table tennis and squash to 11, tennis and padel with deuce or golden point and tie-breaks, sets for volleyball, throwball and sepak takraw, halves or quarters with penalties, shoot-outs or overtime for football, futsal, hockey, kabaddi and basketball, frames (or ball by ball) for snooker and pool, and boards for carrom. You can change match length and points for club formats, or just enter the result at the end.

Can I split an Open event by level?

Yes. Tag each player's level (one tap per player, or when adding them) and choose Split by level: one pool per level, whose winners meet in the semis, or 2 pools (lower and upper levels) that meet in the final. You can swap the semi pairings, move a player to another group until it starts, and put a spot entry straight into the right group.

How do teams and per-player payments work?

A team's captain enters it with a list of players, and the captain (or you) can edit the team any time: add each player's email and the team's results go on their Tonament profile. For fees, mark each player as paid or waived, and the team shows as paid once everyone is settled. Team events can also charge per player instead of per team. Pairs work the same way: mark each partner's half.

What if a player turns up late?

Before the draw, tick players as they arrive — only ticked players are drawn. Late for their match? Tap Not here: their matches wait and the court takes the next one, and Here ✓ brings them back (or give the walkover if they never come). Entered but missed the draw? Tap Arrived and they join a group with catch-up matches, or take a bye in a knockout. New walk-ins join the same way with late entries. A player who's injured mid-match can retire, and a pair can change partner with Rename. If several players arrive before play starts, just redo the draw. Set a late-arrival policy in Setup › Late arrivals and players see it; on the day, Needs a decision tells you when a walkover is due.

On the day

How does the AI assistant work, and is it fair?

On a tournament's Players or Live tab, tell the assistant what's happening — typed, dictated or a photo of the sign-up sheet. It suggests changes from a short list (check in, arrived, late, back, add an entry, paid, walkover, pair two players, a walk-in in place of a no-show), each with what it will do and why. Nothing changes until you tick and apply, and you can undo for 10 minutes. Where a latecomer goes and when a walkover is due follow your published late-arrival policy, worked out from group sizes and matches played — never from who the players are. The AI never decides groups, seeds or results, and never sees players' phone numbers or emails.

What do AI credits cost?

AI help is paid per tournament, once: AI setup (posters, forms and Google Form imports) ₹99, AI on the day (the assistant) ₹199, or both ₹249 — the Championship plan includes both. Your first AI setup is free, and a friend you invite gets their first one free too. A failed AI request doesn't use credits, and everything in Tonament still works without AI.

How do umpires score?

Add umpires by email, or send them a scorer link. They open a match and tap the side that won each rally; Tonament keeps the score, the server and game points. A mistake is one tap on Undo.

What if the venue Wi-Fi drops?

Scoring keeps working. Points are saved on the umpire's phone and sent as soon as the connection is back, and two phones can never overwrite each other's score.

How does the order of play work?

Set up your courts and the Courts tab shows what's on each court and what's next across every event, with estimated start times. Call a match to a free court in one tap, or switch on auto-call. Players can search for their name to see when they're on.

Sharing and visibility

Who can see my tournament?

You choose: Private (only your team), Link only (anyone with the link can view and enter) or Public (also listed on Tonament). New tournaments start private.

Will my tournament show up on Google?

Public tournaments can — each has its own page with the dates, venue and events, set up so Google can list it as an event. Private and link-only tournaments are never listed.

Can I add my logo and sponsors?

Yes, with the Open plan or above: your logo, name and colours on the public pages, and sponsor logos, with "Powered by Tonament" at the bottom. Every plan can add a cover image and photos.

Payments

Do I get anything for inviting other organisers?

Yes. Share your invite link (Account menu → Invite organisers). The organiser you invite gets their first tournament free up to 64 entries (the Club plan). Once they run it with 10 or more entries, you get a free Club upgrade for any of your tournaments — and an Open upgrade for every 3rd friend. If you have an academy or venue page, it gets a month more too.

How do I pay, and can I get a refund?

Plans are paid once per tournament, securely through Razorpay (UPI, cards, netbanking, wallets). See the refund policy for when refunds apply.
