How Tonament works

Run your tournament. Score it live. Tonament takes a club tournament from entries to the final — online entries, draws, court schedules and live scores on everyone's phone. No app to install.

Create a tournament Find a tournament

For organisers

  1. 1

    Create your tournament

    Three quick taps: pick the sport, pick your events — Men's Singles, Mixed Doubles, U19, teams — then the name and date. A checklist on your tournament page walks you through the rest when you're ready: venue, format, entry fee, prizes, courts and publishing.

  2. 2

    Open entries and share the link

    Your tournament gets its own page with the dates, venue, prizes and what's included. Share the link on WhatsApp or Instagram — it shows a proper preview — and players enter online. Charging an entry fee? Players pay you by UPI and you confirm each payment with a tap.

  3. 3

    Manage entries

    See who's in, confirm places from the waitlist, pair up players looking for a doubles partner, and tick players in as they arrive. List order is the seeding.

  4. 4

    Make the draw

    Choose round-robin groups that feed a knockout, a league, or a straight knockout — the groups, fixtures and bracket are made in one tap, with byes for top seeds.

  5. 5

    Run the courts

    Set up your courts and Tonament keeps one order of play across every event: call the next match to a free court in one tap, or let it call matches automatically. Players in two events are never called to two courts at once.

  6. 6

    Score it live

    Umpires score on their own phones — tap the side that won the rally. The right rules are built in for each sport, scores appear live for everyone, and standings and the bracket update themselves.

For players

  1. 1

    Find a tournament

    Search by sport, city, date or event on the home page — or open the link the organiser shared.

    2

    Sign in and enter

    Create a free account once, then enter in seconds: your name is filled in, add a partner for doubles — or ask to be paired with someone.

    3

    Follow your matches

    On the day, see the order of play, when you're likely on and which court, and follow every point live.

    4

    Keep track

    "My entries" on the home page shows every tournament you've entered and where your entry stands. Plans changed? Withdraw before the draw.
