  1. Entries

    Entries that run themselves

    Share one link on WhatsApp and players enter from their phones — singles, doubles, mixed, age groups and levels. No partner? They ask to be paired. Full events fill a waitlist, and players see their own entries and can withdraw.

    Charging a fee? Players pay you directly by UPI and you confirm each payment with one tap. Tonament never touches the money.

  2. Live scoring

    Every point, live

    Umpires score on their own phones: tap the side that won the rally. Each sport's rules are built in — the server, game point, deuce, tie-breaks, changing ends.

    Scores reach every phone in seconds, standings and brackets update themselves, and scoring keeps working if the venue Wi-Fi drops.

  3. New · Venue display

    Every court on the big screen

    Open one link on any TV or projector: live scores on every court, who's up next with estimated times, and results and standings in rotation. Players stop crowding the desk asking when they're on.

Everything in Tonament

Entries & players

  • Online entries with free player accounts and prefilled forms
  • Many events in one tournament: singles, doubles, mixed, teams, age groups, levels
  • Partner finder for solo players, or draw doubles for a social night
  • Waitlist and maximum places per event
  • Check-in on the day, and late entries with catch-up matches
  • My entries for players, with withdraw before the draw

Entry fees

  • UPI straight to you: the player's UPI app opens with the amount filled in
  • Your QR code on the payment screen
  • One-tap confirm: Received, Not received, Paid in cash or Waive
  • Places held for 24 h, 48 h or until entries close while unpaid

Draws & formats

  • League + knockout, league only or knockout only, per event
  • Seeding by list order, with byes for top seeds
  • Groups and fixtures made in one tap; redo any time before play
  • Match length and points set per event

On the day

  • Courts and order of play across every event, with estimated start times
  • Call matches in one tap, or let Tonament call them automatically
  • No double-booking: players in two events are never on two courts
  • Umpires and scorer links; mistakes are one tap on Undo
  • Venue display for any TV or projector

Your tournament page

  • A page of its own with dates, times, venue, events and results
  • Prizes and what's included: cash, trophies, shuttles, T-shirts, food
  • Link previews that look right on WhatsApp and Instagram
  • Found on Google when it's public; private or link-only when it's not
  • Your logo, colours and sponsors (Open plan and above)

For players & spectators
