  1. Entries

    Entries that run themselves

    Share one link on WhatsApp and players enter from their phones — singles, doubles, mixed, age groups and levels. No partner? They ask to be paired. Full events fill a waitlist, and players see their own entries and can withdraw.

    Charging a fee? Players pay you directly by UPI and you confirm each payment with one tap. Tonament never touches the money.

  2. Live scoring

    Every point, live

    Umpires score on their own phones: tap the side that won the rally. Each sport's rules are built in — the server, game point, deuce, tie-breaks, changing ends.

    Scores reach every phone in seconds, standings and brackets update themselves, and scoring keeps working if the venue Wi-Fi drops.

  3. New · Split by level

    One Open event. Every level.

    Beginners and state-level players in the same Open? Tag each entry's level and split the event: each level plays its own groups, and the level winners meet in the semis — or two pools, lower and upper levels, that meet in the final. Just like your paper draw.

    You still make the calls: move a player to the right group until they've played, swap the semi pairings, and put a spot entry straight into the group they belong in.

  4. New · On the day

    Late players? No problem.

    Tap Not here and their match waits while the next ready match goes on court; Here ✓ brings them back. Never came? Give the walkover. Injured mid-match? Retire, with the score kept.

    A substitute partner is a rename. A pair who entered but missed the draw joins a group with catch-up matches, and walk-ins pay by UPI or cash at the desk — the order of play adjusts itself every time.

    Running the day, step by step →

  5. New · Groups

    Your daily games, sorted

    For the people you play with every day. Share one link in your WhatsApp group: players tap "I'm in" and say when they're coming, so everyone sees how many will be there and when.

    At the court they check in and get fair games picked for them — fewest games first, balanced teams, new partners. Score live or add the result at the end; the other side confirms it, so there are no arguments, and every confirmed game moves the group leaderboard.

    Venues and academies post daily slots by level — Beginners at 6, Intermediates at 7 — on their own page. Players join in one tap, and the attendance tab shows who actually turned up.

  6. New · Venue display

    Every court on the big screen

    Open one link on any TV or projector: live scores on every court, who's up next with estimated times, and results and standings in rotation. Players stop crowding the desk asking when they're on.
