Delivery of the Event Pass

  • The Event Pass is delivered digitally: it is activated on your tournament immediately after your payment succeeds.
  • You'll see "Event Pass active" in the tournament, and a receipt is sent to the email address on your account.
  • If your Pass isn't active within 1 hour of a successful payment, contact us with your payment ID and we'll activate it manually.
