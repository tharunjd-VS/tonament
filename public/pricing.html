Questions

How do I buy an Event Pass?

Create your tournament for free. When you need more than 16 entries, choose Get Event Pass in the tournament. Payment is handled by Razorpay, and the Pass is activated on your tournament as soon as the payment succeeds. You'll also get a receipt by email.

Is it a subscription?

No. The Event Pass is a one-time payment for one tournament. There are no recurring charges.

Are taxes included?

The price shown is the total amount you pay, inclusive of any applicable taxes.
