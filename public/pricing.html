Questions

How do I buy a plan?

Create your tournament for free. When you need more than 16 entries, choose a plan in the tournament. Payment is handled by Razorpay, and the plan is activated on your tournament as soon as the payment succeeds. You'll also get a receipt by email.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. If your tournament outgrows its plan, move up to a bigger one and pay only the difference — for example, Club to Open costs ₹500.

Is it a subscription?

No. Each plan is a one-time payment for one tournament. There are no recurring charges.

I bought the Event Pass before

Tournaments that bought the original Event Pass keep unlimited entries.

Are taxes included?

The price shown is the total amount you pay, inclusive of any applicable taxes.
