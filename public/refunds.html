Cancellation & Refund Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

This policy covers the Event Pass, a one-time payment that unlocks unlimited entries for one tournament.

Refunds

  • Before your tournament starts (before the draw is made), you can request a full refund within 7 days of payment.
  • After the tournament has started, the Event Pass has been used and is not refundable.
  • If you were charged twice or a payment failed but money was deducted, we'll refund the extra or failed amount in full.

How to request a refund

Email us with your tournament name and Razorpay payment ID (on your receipt). Approved refunds are made to the original payment method within 5–7 working days; the time it takes to appear depends on your bank.

Cancellation

The Event Pass is not a subscription, so there is nothing to cancel: there are no recurring charges. When a refund is issued, the tournament returns to the free plan.
