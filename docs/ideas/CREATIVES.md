# AI-generated promo creatives (idea, not built yet)

> **Status:** concept, for discussion. Nothing here is built.
> **Started:** 22 Oct 2026.
> **Decided (22 Oct 2026):**
> - Creatives are **generated by AI**, not hand-made templates. The app takes the activity's details and the organiser's branding and produces promo images and videos.
> - Users pay with **credits**.

## How AI generation should work (hybrid, so details are always right)

AI image and video models are great at artwork, atmosphere and style. They're unreliable at exact text, logos, scores, dates, QR codes and faces of real players, and a poster with a wrong date or a mangled sponsor logo is worse than none. So each creative is made in three layers:

1. **AI art direction (a language model).** Given the moment (e.g. "Champions, Men's Doubles"), the tournament data and the brand, an LLM:
   - picks the concept, layout, hierarchy and copy (headline, tagline);
   - writes the **layout itself as HTML/SVG**, a fresh design every time, not a fixed template;
   - writes the **caption and hashtags** for the post.
2. **AI artwork (an image or video model), optional.** It generates the background or hero art: a stylised shuttle-and-court scene, a trophy, dramatic lighting, in the organiser's colours. It never draws the text, logos, QR codes or real faces.
3. **Exact overlay (code).** The real data is placed precisely:
   - names, dates, venue, fees, scores and draws, straight from Tonament;
   - logos and sponsor logos as uploaded;
   - a QR code to the tournament;
   - real player photos (from public profiles only).

   The result is rendered to PNG, or to MP4 for video, at Story, Square and Landscape sizes.

The organiser sees 3–4 variations, taps one, can say "make it more festive" or "use our blue" (another generation) and shares. Text edits are free and instant, because the text is real text, not pixels.

**Videos:**
- **Cheaper:** motion layouts animated from layers 1 and 3 (like the 22 Oct promo video).
- **Premium:** AI-generated video clips from a video model as backgrounds or intros.

## Credits

Generation costs real money (LLM and image or video model calls), so it's priced in credits:

| Item | Credits (example) |
|---|---|
| Image creative (3–4 variations, all sizes) | 1 |
| Re-style / "make it more …" | 1 |
| Caption + hashtags only | free with any creative |
| Motion video (animated layout, 10–20 s) | 3 |
| AI video (generated footage) | 10 |

**Where credits come from:**
- **Credit packs,** bought with Razorpay, which already handles one-time purchases. Example: 10 credits ₹199, 50 credits ₹799, 200 credits ₹2,499.
- **Credits included with paid plans,** e.g.:
  - Open: 10;
  - Championship: 25;
  - academy and venue page: 50 a year.
- **A few free credits for new organisers** (e.g. 3) so they try it.
- **Free, no credits:** the plain link-preview card and player share cards, because they're the growth loop (Tonament-branded).

**How it's run:**
- **Price above model cost.** Set credit prices so even the most expensive generation (AI video) keeps a margin; track the real cost per generation in the admin dashboard.
- **Accounting:** a credit ledger per account (bought, included, spent, refunded on failed generations); the admin dashboard shows usage and revenue.
- **Failed or blocked generations** are refunded automatically.

## Guardrails
- **Facts come only from Tonament data.** The model never writes dates, fees or names on its own; the overlay does.
- **Player photos** only from public profiles, and never altered by AI.
- **Safety:** content filters on prompts and outputs; no other brands' logos; organisers confirm they own the logos they upload (already required for branding).
- **Records:** keep the prompt and inputs of each creative so it can be regenerated or reported.


## The idea

Tonament already holds everything a promo needs: the tournament name, dates, venue, events, entry fee, prizes, entries, the draw, the order of play, live scores, results, champions, player photos, organiser logo and sponsors. Groups and academies will add sessions, ratings and leaderboards.

So every moment can produce a **ready-to-post image or short video for WhatsApp Status, Instagram and Facebook, in one tap**, with no Canva and no designer. The organiser, venue or group gets better promotion. Tonament gets its brand on every post.

## Is there potential? Yes, for three reasons

1. **Organisers spend real time on this today.** A typical tournament means 5–10 posters:
   - announcement;
   - "last few slots";
   - fixtures and the order of play;
   - semi-finals;
   - winners;
   - sponsor thank-yous.

   Each one is made by hand in Canva, re-typing details from a spreadsheet, usually late at night, and with typos.
2. **The data is already structured and correct.** Generic tools (Canva, Instagram templates) need every detail typed in. Sports apps handle bookings, not tournament-moment creatives (as far as I know). Generation from live data is the gap.
3. **It's free distribution.** Every shared card carries "tonament.com". Players share "I'm in the semi-final", "Champion" and "#1 in Chennai" cards, and each share reaches people who play. This is the strongest growth loop in the whole product.

## Risk of over-complicating, and how to avoid it

- **Videos come later.** Start with images: they're 80% of the value at 20% of the effort. Short videos (scores and winners animating) come after the image templates prove used.
- **AI does the design; code does the facts** (see "How AI generation should work"). Accuracy never depends on the model.
- **No design editor.** You get variations plus "make it more …" requests and free text tweaks. A full editor is a trap.
- **Rendering:** images are rendered in the phone's browser or a small server function. Video rendering and AI video run as background jobs, since Vercel functions are short-lived: a queue, with the user notified when ready.

## The moments (each one gets a one-tap "Share" card)

### Tournaments (organisers, academies, venues)

| Moment | Card |
|---|---|
| Entries open | Poster: name, date, venue, events, fee, prizes, "Enter at tonament.com/t/…" + QR |
| Countdown / filling up | "3 days to go", "Only 6 places left in Men's Doubles" |
| Draw made | The fixtures / group tables for an event |
| Match day | Today's order of play by court |
| Live | "Match point on Court 2", "Semi-final now live" (+ live link) |
| Results | Results of the day; a semi-final line-up |
| Champions | A winner poster per event: player photos, score, trophy, sponsor logos |
| Thank you | Sponsors and venue; "See you next year" |

### Players (personal, the viral loop)
- **Before and during:** "I'm entered in …", "Through to the quarter-finals".
- **Wins:** "Champion — Men's Doubles", "Best of 2026 · 4 titles".
- **Standings:** "#3 in Chennai, Women's Singles", "New rating high: 1,184".

### Groups (when Groups are built)
- **Sessions:** a session summary ("24 games today · Asha unbeaten · best partnership Ravi & Kiran"), "Tomorrow 6 am · 4 courts · RSVP".
- **Weekly:** the leaderboard, player of the week, a group-vs-group result.

### Academies and venues
- **Academies:** admissions open, new batch timings, coaching programmes, student achievements ("3 of our players in the district final").
- **Venues:** open-play slots this week, upcoming tournaments here, the hall of champions.

## Formats
- **Images:** Story (1080×1920), Square (1080×1080) and Landscape (1200×630, which is also the link preview).
- **Later, videos:** 10–20 s of motion templates, e.g. a score ticker, a winners reveal, "this week at the venue". Rendered in the browser to MP4 or WebM, with music added by the user in Instagram or WhatsApp.

## Revenue: credits, and a revenue platform for organisers

- **For organisers, venues and academies:**
  - **Sponsor inventory:** sponsor logos on every creative (announcement, fixtures, live, winners) give organisers something concrete to sell to sponsors ("your logo on 15 posts and the venue screen").
  - **Fuller tournaments:** better promotion means more entries, which means more entry-fee revenue.
  - **Academies:** admissions and achievement posts bring in students.
- **For Tonament:**

  **Credits** (see above). Paid plans include some credits and carry the organiser's own logo, colours and sponsors on creatives; Tonament shrinks to a small "Powered by". This is recurring, usage-based revenue that grows with how actively organisers promote.

## What's already built that this reuses
- **Link previews:** the tournament share image, 1200×630 from the cover (`prepareImage(…, 'preview')`).
- **Branding:** plans with branding (logo, colours, sponsors), the white-label venue display and the brand assets (`docs/BRAND.md`).
- **Data:** live tournament data (`GET /api/tournaments/:id`), profiles, ranking points and the DUPR rating.
- **Earlier experiments:** the hand-made promo images and the 30 s video from 22 Oct 2026 show the look. They were made with the same approach (HTML templates rendered in a browser).

## Rendering: how images and videos get made (notes, 22 Oct 2026)

The promo images and the 30 s video from 22 Oct were HTML pages. Images were captured with a headless browser (Chromium screenshots); the video was rendered frame by frame (900 frames) and joined with ffmpeg. The app can do the same:

| Where | How | Good for | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phone (browser) | Layout drawn to a canvas, saved as PNG, shared with the share sheet straight to WhatsApp / Instagram | Free player share cards, instant previews | Small differences between phones; in-browser video recording varies by phone |
| Server: Satori + resvg | HTML-like layout → SVG → PNG in milliseconds (the approach Vercel uses for link-preview images) | Fast, cheap images | Supports only part of CSS |
| Server: headless Chromium | A slimmed-down Chromium on Vercel; pixel-perfect and identical everywhere | AI-designed HTML posters | A second or two per image |
| Render worker (background job) | Chromium + ffmpeg on a small separate worker; "your video is ready" notification | Videos (motion and AI) | Needs a worker outside Vercel's function time limits |

**Plan:**
- AI posters are rendered on the server (consistent everywhere);
- videos are rendered by a background worker;
- free player share cards are rendered on the phone.

**Prototype to try first:** a "Share poster" button on a tournament that makes an "Entries open" image from its real details.
