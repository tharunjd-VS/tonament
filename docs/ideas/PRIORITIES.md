# What will get people hooked: an honest assessment (22 Oct 2026)

## The core truth: habit comes from frequency
- **Tournaments are rare.** An organiser runs a few a year; a keen player enters one every month or two. However good the tournament tools are, they're opened only occasionally.
- **What happens every day:** casual-competitive play. The morning group, the club night, the academy batch. Whatever owns that daily moment becomes the app people open every day.

## The ideas so far, ranked by how much they'll hook people

| Idea | Hook | Honest take |
|---|---|---|
| **Groups: daily RSVP, fair rotation, live scores, a rating that moves every game** | ★★★★★ | The strongest idea. It solves a felt, daily pain (WhatsApp RSVP chaos, waiting, score arguments) and creates a rating that changes every day — the loop that keeps people on chess.com and Strava. Everything else (profiles, rankings, tournaments) gets its data from here. |
| **Player share cards** (champion, ranked #3, new rating high, entered) | ★★★★ | Cheap to build, needs no AI, and it's the growth engine: every share is a free ad to people who play. Make it free and early. |
| **Live scoring at tournaments + venue display** | ★★★★ (acquisition) / ★★ (habit) | The "wow" that wins organisers and shows players Tonament. Done well already. But it's episodic: it brings people in, it doesn't keep them. |
| **Profiles + tournament ranking points** | ★★★ (once there's data) | Only feels alive with lots of results. Empty rankings look dead. Daily group games fill profiles fast; tournaments alone won't. |
| **Group vs group, inter-group leagues, groups entering tournaments** | ★★★★ (later) | Powerful network effects and a fix for the re-entry problem, but it needs many active groups first. Phase 2. |
| **AI promo creatives (credits)** | ★★ (habit) / ★★★ (revenue) | Real value for organisers, and monetisable. But it doesn't create a habit, and organisers can already get close with Canva + ChatGPT. What makes ours better is "it already knows everything". Build after Groups; start with the free share cards. |
| **Academy / venue pages (paid)** | ★★ now / ★★★★ later | A sound business model, but it sells only once Tonament sends real traffic. Today there's little to show an academy. Keep it, don't push it yet. |
| **DUPR** | ★ | Niche in India today. Fine as a checkbox for pickleball, not a hook. |

## The main risk: too many features, too few users
The product is already broad (10 sports, tournaments, payments, plans, academy pages, rankings, DUPR, admin). More features won't create users. **A few groups using it every day will.** The next win is depth in one loop, not breadth.

## Recommendation: focus for the next 4–6 weeks
1. **Fix login friction:** an email magic link with the phone remembered. A 5:30 am RSVP must be one tap.
2. **Groups MVP:** a recurring session, an RSVP link for WhatsApp, check-in, the next-game suggestion, live scoring by players with the loser confirming, a rating and a leaderboard. Nothing else.
3. **Dogfood it in your own badminton group every day.** Fix what annoys people within the week.
4. **Free player and group share cards:** "Asha unbeaten today", "#1 this week".
5. **Grow by hand to 10 groups** in Chennai (friends' groups, the venues you play at).
6. **Measure:**
   - weekly active players;
   - games scored per day;
   - the share of RSVPs made in the app instead of on WhatsApp;
   - shares per week.
7. **Then:** groups enter tournaments in one tap → group-vs-group challenges → AI creatives with credits → paid venue and academy tools.

## The loop we want

```
WhatsApp RSVP link → play → live score, no arguments → rating moves
        ↑                                                     ↓
friends join the group ← share card ("#1 this week") ← leaderboard
        ↓
group challenges another group → tournaments (entered in one tap) → organisers pay
```
